Best for specific needs

Best for rugged protection

Its dual-layer build absorbs shock from waist-height falls, and the raised edges shield your screen even if it lands face-down. Pair it with a tempered glass screen protector for peace of mind. For seamless daily use with chargers, wallets, and stands, the Apple Silicone Case with MagSafe is the standard. Best for style and individuality

The Casetify Impact Case lets you turn your iPhone into a canvas with thousands of designs and personalization options. Our verdict

No single case fits every person, but the OtterBox Commuter Series earns the top spot because it does almost everything well: solid drop protection, a comfortable grip, and no big downsides. If your priority is saving money, grab the Spigen Tough Armor; if MagSafe is central to your setup, Apple’s own case is a no-brainer. And for those who want a case that shows off personality as much as it shields the phone, Casetify delivers. No matter which you choose, invest in a quality case, your iPhone will thank you.
